:::problem The Problem & The Approach
**The problem.** Microsoft builds great products, but its web presence often lacks the
cohesion of competitors like Apple or Google. The main site is the clearest example:
compiling marketing for every product at once creates bloat, and a cluttered or slow
page can easily drive a visitor away.

---

**The approach.** Redesign one surface at a time, starting with the main website, and
write a short case study for each explaining the decisions. Everyone already knows what
Microsoft does, a rare advantage, so the redesign uses that recognition to simplify
the journey instead of explaining everything up front.
:::

## Why Microsoft

Having used Microsoft products for personal and professional work throughout my career,
I appreciate how powerful the ecosystem is. While Microsoft rightly prioritizes
enterprise workflows, there's room to bring a more polished, consumer-grade feel to
everyday users, and bringing that same design quality to enterprise tools would raise
the bar there too. Design is the first thing people see.
